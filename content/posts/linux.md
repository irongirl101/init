Oh boy. The one that started it all. Linux. 
~how can i not highlight linux when it is its birthday week XP~

Everyone here (I hope) has an idea of what Linux is, and how peak of an Operating System it is. Instead of going in depth on what the repo contains, i wanna highlight on some fun facts about Linux. 
- Way back in the 90s, on RedHat specifically, there was a config/setup to attempt an auto detect of the sound card and then play a sound sample. It played Linus Torvalds actually saying, "I am Linus Trovalds, and I pronounce Linux as 'Linux' "  ![Listen to it here](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/03/Linus-linux.ogg)
- At its peak (2.6), there were around 70 F-bombs in the kernel code, its now come down to just 20 D:  [lookie here for some fun stats on funni words, including 'penguin' ![Penguin](https://www.vidarholen.net/contents/wordcount/) or for 'shit' - ![Shit](https://github.com/search?q=repo%3Atorvalds%2Flinux+shit&type=code) or ![FBomb](https://github.com/search?q=repo%3Atorvalds%2Flinux+fuck&type=code) ]
- Literally EVERYTHING is a file -- including stuff like the activity of mouse, speaker, and monitor; you can probably do some very funky stuff XP ![like this](https://www.reddit.com/r/thinkpad/comments/dcay0w/i_wrote_a_python_script_that_flashes_the_lid_led/)
- It has *insult* support: If you use sudo visudo to open the sudo config file and add `Defaults insults` then every time you incorrectly type your sudo password, it gives you a random insult. (You might need to restart your session) 
- There were plans to call Linux as  'FreaX' 
- you can -rm a running file on linux 
Theres so much more cool stuff, which you can find on Linux, which i possibly cannot fit here. I encourage yall to look for more easter eggs, and some fun stuff Linux can do!
