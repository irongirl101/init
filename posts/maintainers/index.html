Published on: 2026-05-08

In honor of May-ntainer Month (and wishin y'all a belated may the fourth), i'll be talking about maintainers and how pivotal they are to the open source world.

Who even is a maintainer?¶

An open-source software maintainer is a key contributor responsible for directing a project, reviewing pull requests, managing releases, and handling security and building a community.
Maintainers often focus more on stewardship and strategic decisions than writing code, frequently managing burnout through established project governance.

But being a maintainer is not just glory - they often end up doing a lot of fixing of ci/cd pipelines, getting burnouts from the insane number of PRs they might get (if they maintain a huge OS project).

Key Responsibilities of a Maintainer:¶

  • Code Review & Quality
  • Security & Stability
  • Community Management
  • Strategic Direction

A maintainer shapes the project to what it is - and you can be a maintainer as well, even for your own projects that probably three people know about. It’s about taking ownership and building something that lasts.

The burnout gets real - so when your next PR gets merged, make sure to tell thank you (and be a good contributor)!
