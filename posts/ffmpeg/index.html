Open Source Friday #11 - FFmpeg

Published on: 2026-06-19

Tags: multimedia commands OSF

Continuing the series of giants in the open source space, we cannot go ahead without talking about such an amazing application.
Ffmpeg is a collection of libraries and tools to process multimedia content such as audio, video, subtitles and related metadata. It is literally a Swiss Army Knife for digital media, enabling you to perform any function on any media file.

What can it do?¶

  • Transcoding - Converts from one format to another
  • Editing - Trimming, splitting, or stitching multiple videos together.
  • Extracting - Stripping the audio out of a video or capturing high-quality video frames as images.
  • Optimization - Compressing file sizes, altering bitrates, and resizing video dimensions.
  • and streaming too!

But why ffmpeg over anything like Adobe Premier or any other video editing software?¶

ffmpeg uses text commands. This allows users to write scripts that automate heavy, repetitive video processing tasks instantly; rather than using heavy GUI based applications.

A couple of tools include¶

  • ffplay
  • ffprobe
  • libraries for low level dev libs like libavcodec and libavformat, that other softwares use to interact with media

It is widely considered the industry standard for multimedia processing and serves as the hidden engine behind major platforms like YouTube, Netflix, and OBS.
